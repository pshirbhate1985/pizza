Pizza

Pizza is a universally loved Italian dish, characterized by a round, baked dough base topped with savory tomato sauce, melted cheese, and various toppings.It originated in the Naples area of Southern Italy.Pizza is a favored meal for parties, social gatherings, and casual dining with friends or family. With countless variations, it remains one of the most loved comfort foods globally.It is a savory, mouthwatering food enjoyed by people of all ages.
